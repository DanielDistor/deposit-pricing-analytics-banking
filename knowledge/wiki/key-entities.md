# Key Entities: Banks, Products, and Terminology

## Bank Profiles

### Online Banks (High Pass-Through)

**Marcus by Goldman Sachs**
- Type: Online bank (subsidiary of Goldman Sachs)
- Savings APY: ~4.10% (as of early 2025, after 2024 cuts)
- CD rates: Competitive 1-year CDs around 4.50–4.75% at peak (2023)
- Pass-through reputation: HIGH — consistently among the top rates in the market
- Key characteristic: No physical branches; competes entirely on rate and digital experience
- History: Launched 2016; grew rapidly during 2022–2023 hike cycle by offering top-tier APYs

**Ally Bank**
- Type: Online bank (formerly GMAC Bank, rebranded 2009)
- Savings APY: ~4.00–4.20% (after 2024 cuts)
- CD rates: 1-year CDs ~4.50% at peak (2023)
- Pass-through reputation: HIGH — pioneer of online banking; known for transparent, competitive rates
- Key characteristic: No fees, no minimums; strong mobile app
- History: One of the first major online banks; built customer trust through the 2022–2023 cycle

**SoFi Bank**
- Type: Fintech/online bank
- Savings APY: ~4.30–4.50% (with direct deposit; varies by membership status)
- Pass-through reputation: HIGH — offers bonus rates for members with direct deposit
- Key characteristic: Bundled financial services (banking, investing, loans); rate tied to membership perks
- History: Originally a student loan company; obtained bank charter in 2022; aggressive rate offerings

### Traditional Banks (Low Pass-Through)

**JPMorgan Chase**
- Type: Traditional bank (largest U.S. bank by assets)
- Savings APY: ~0.01% (standard savings account)
- Pass-through reputation: VERY LOW — maintained near-zero deposit rates throughout 2022–2023 hiking cycle
- Key characteristic: 4,700+ branches, extensive ATM network, dominant market position
- Why low pass-through: Massive sticky deposit base; customers stay for convenience and trust, not rate

**Bank of America**
- Type: Traditional bank (2nd largest U.S. bank)
- Savings APY: ~0.01–0.04% (Advantage Savings)
- Pass-through reputation: VERY LOW — similar to Chase
- Key characteristic: Large retail footprint, Merrill Lynch wealth management integration

**Wells Fargo**
- Type: Traditional bank (3rd largest U.S. bank)
- Savings APY: ~0.01% (Way2Save)
- Pass-through reputation: VERY LOW
- Key characteristic: Way2Save automatic transfer feature; large branch network; $2,500 minimum for CDs

**Citibank**
- Type: Traditional bank (4th largest U.S. bank)
- Savings APY: ~0.04% (standard savings)
- Pass-through reputation: VERY LOW
- Key characteristic: Strong international presence; Citi Accelerate Savings offers higher rates in select markets

## Product Definitions

### Savings Account
- **What it is**: A deposit account at a bank or credit union that earns interest
- **Liquidity**: High — funds accessible anytime (historically limited to 6 withdrawals/month; Reg D relaxed post-2020)
- **Rate type**: Variable — bank can change APY at any time
- **FDIC insured**: Yes, up to $250,000
- **Best for**: Emergency funds, short-term savings goals

### Certificate of Deposit (CD)
- **What it is**: A time deposit with a fixed term and fixed APY
- **Liquidity**: Low — early withdrawal triggers a penalty (typically 3–12 months of interest)
- **Rate type**: Fixed for the term
- **Common terms**: 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, 2 years, 5 years
- **FDIC insured**: Yes, up to $250,000
- **Best for**: Savers who won't need the money during the term; locking in high rates before Fed cuts

### Money Market Account (MMA)
- **What it is**: Hybrid savings/checking account with higher APY
- **Liquidity**: Medium — typically includes check-writing and debit card access
- **Rate type**: Variable
- **FDIC insured**: Yes
- **Best for**: Savers who want liquidity plus a better rate than standard savings

## Rate Terminology Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| APY | Annual Percentage Yield — effective annual return accounting for compounding frequency |
| APR | Annual Percentage Rate — simple annual rate without compounding (used more for loans) |
| Basis Points (bps) | Unit = 0.01%; 100 bps = 1.00%; 525 bps = 5.25% |
| Spread | Gap between two rates (e.g., Fed rate − deposit APY) |
| Pass-Through Ratio | % of Fed rate change passed to depositors: (ΔAPY / ΔFed Rate) × 100 |
| Rate Cycle | Hiking (rising rates), Cutting (falling rates), or Hold |
| FDIC | Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation — insures bank deposits up to $250K |
| FOMC | Federal Open Market Committee — Fed body that sets the funds rate target |
| Net Interest Margin | Bank spread between earning asset yields and funding costs |
| Sticky Deposits | Deposits unlikely to leave regardless of rate (loyal, inertia-driven customers) |
| Online Bank | Bank without physical branches; competes primarily on rate and digital UX |
| Traditional Bank | Bank with physical branch network; competes on service and convenience |
