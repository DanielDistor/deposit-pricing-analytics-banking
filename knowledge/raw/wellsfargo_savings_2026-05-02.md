# Wells Fargo Savings Accounts and CDs

Source: https://www.wellsfargo.com/savings-cds/
Scraped: 2026-05-02

## Savings Products Offered

### Way2Save Savings (Most Popular)
- Monthly service fee: $5 (avoidable)
- Minimum opening deposit: $25
- Feature: Save As You Go — automatic $1 transfer per debit card purchase
- APY: Near 0% (typical of traditional bank savings)

### Platinum Savings
- Monthly service fee: $12 (waived with $3,500 minimum daily balance)
- Minimum opening deposit: $25
- Feature: Option to write checks from savings account

### Wells Fargo CDs
- Minimum opening deposit: $2,500
- Terms: 3 months to 1 year or more
- No monthly service fee
- Early withdrawal penalties apply (1–12 months interest depending on term)
- FDIC insured

## Key Observations for Deposit Pricing Analysis

Wells Fargo is one of the "Big Four" traditional banks (Chase, BofA, Wells Fargo, Citibank). Its savings account rates remain near 0% despite the Federal Reserve hiking rates from 0% to 5.25–5.50% between 2022–2023. This is the textbook example of **low pass-through**: Wells Fargo retained the rate spread as margin rather than passing it to depositors.

- Traditional bank advantage: large branch network, brand trust, sticky customer relationships
- Traditional bank disadvantage: near-zero savings APY vs. 4%+ at online banks
- A $100,000 depositor choosing Wells Fargo over an online bank earns ~$4,000–$5,000 less per year at peak rates

See wellsfargo.com/savings-cds/rates/ for current rate tables.
