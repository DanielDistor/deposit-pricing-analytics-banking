## Products Offered

Chase offers three savings account options:

### 1. Chase Savings℠ (Most Popular)
- **Monthly Service Fee:** $5, or $0 with qualifying conditions
- **Minimum deposit:** $0
- **Features:**
  - Earns interest (variable rate — see current rates separately)
  - Autosave: automatic transfers from Chase checking account
  - Access to 14,000 ATMs and 5,000 branches
  - Online & mobile banking via Chase Mobile® app

### 2. Chase Premier Savings℠
- **Monthly Service Fee:** $25, or $0 with qualifying conditions
- **Features:**
  - Earn Premier relationship rates when linked to Chase Premier Plus Checking℠ or Chase Sapphire® Banking
  - Requires at least 5 customer-initiated transactions monthly in linked checking

### 3. Chase Private Client Savings℠
- **Monthly Service Fee:** $0
- **Features:**
  - For existing Chase Private Client Checking℠ customers
  - Earn relationship rates
  - For high-net-worth clients

## Interest Rate Context

Chase's savings account interest rate is variable and determined daily at Chase's discretion, subject to change without notice. Based on comparison data from other sources:

- **Chase Savings APY: 0.01%** (as of late April 2026, per Ally comparison data)
- **Chase Savings APY: 0.02%** (as of May 2026, per Marcus comparison data)

This rate is dramatically below online bank competitors:
- Marcus: 3.50% (175x Chase's rate)
- Ally: 3.10%
- SoFi: 3.30%+
- National Average: 0.38–0.58% (still far above Chase)

## Why Chase Pays Low Rates

Chase is the largest U.S. bank by assets (serves nearly 84 million consumers and 7 million small businesses). Traditional large banks like Chase maintain low savings rates because:

1. **Massive branch network** creates high fixed costs funded partly by the spread between deposit rates paid and rates charged on loans
2. **Captive customer base** — customers value branch access, brand trust, and product bundling (checking, mortgage, auto, investing all in one place)
3. **Less competitive pressure** from depositors who don't comparison-shop rates
4. **Relationship banking model** — rates are linked to overall relationship value (Private Client gets better rates)

## Fees Comparison

Chase charges fees that online banks don't:
- $5/month fee on Chase Savings℠ (waivable)
- $25/month fee on Chase Premier Savings℠ (waivable)

Online banks (Ally, Marcus, SoFi) all charge $0 monthly maintenance fees.
