## CD vs. Savings Account

CDs are best for individuals looking for a guaranteed rate of return that's typically higher than a savings account. In exchange for a higher rate, funds are tied up for a set period and early withdrawal penalties may apply. Savings accounts offer more flexibility but variable rates.

## Rate Environment Context

The CD rate environment in 2026 reflects the post-hiking cycle plateau. After the Fed held rates at 5.25–5.50% from July 2023 through late 2024, then cut 75 basis points in Q4 2024, CD rates have been compressing gradually. The best rates in 2023 peaked above 5% APY; by May 2026 the ceiling has dropped to approximately 4.20%.
