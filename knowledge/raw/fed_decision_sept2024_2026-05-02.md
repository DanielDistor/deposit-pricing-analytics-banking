# Federal Reserve FOMC Statement — September 18, 2024

Source: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20240918a.htm
Scraped: 2026-05-02

## Key Decision

The FOMC voted to lower the target range for the federal funds rate by **50 basis points** (0.50 percentage point), from 5.25–5.50% to **4.75–5.00%**. This was the first rate cut since March 2020.

## Economic Context

- Economic activity continued to expand at a solid pace
- Job gains had slowed; unemployment rate had moved up but remained low
- Inflation had made further progress toward the Committee's 2% objective but remained somewhat elevated
- The Committee had "gained greater confidence that inflation is moving sustainably toward 2 percent"

## Forward Guidance

The Committee stated it would "carefully assess incoming data, the evolving outlook, and the balance of risks" when considering additional adjustments.

## Vote

Passed 11–1. Michelle Bowman dissented, preferring a smaller 25 bps cut.

## Significance for Deposit Pricing

This September 2024 cut began the rate-cutting cycle after the 2022–2023 hiking cycle peaked at 5.25–5.50%. Online banks (Marcus, Ally, SoFi) began lowering their savings APYs within days of the announcement. Traditional banks' savings rates barely moved since they had never raised them significantly during the hiking cycle. By end of 2024, the Fed had cut a total of 100 bps (50 bps in Sept, 25 bps in Nov, 25 bps in Dec).
