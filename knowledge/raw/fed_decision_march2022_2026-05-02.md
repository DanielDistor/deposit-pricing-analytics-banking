# Federal Reserve FOMC Statement — March 16, 2022

Source: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20220316a.htm
Scraped: 2026-05-02

## Key Decision

The Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) voted to raise the target range for the federal funds rate from 0–0.25% to **0.25–0.50%** — the first rate increase since December 2018.

## Economic Context

- Economic activity and employment had continued to strengthen
- Job gains were strong; unemployment rate had declined substantially
- Inflation remained elevated, reflecting supply/demand imbalances from the pandemic, higher energy prices, and broader price pressures
- Russia's invasion of Ukraine was expected to create additional upward pressure on inflation

## Forward Guidance

The Committee anticipated "ongoing increases in the target range will be appropriate." It also expected to begin reducing holdings of Treasury securities and agency mortgage-backed securities.

## Vote

Passed 8–1. James Bullard dissented, preferring a 50 bps hike to 0.50–0.75%.

## Significance for Deposit Pricing

This March 2022 decision kicked off the fastest rate-hiking cycle in 40 years. At the time of this first hike, major bank savings APYs were near 0.01%. Online banks (Marcus, Ally, SoFi) would begin raising deposit rates within weeks; traditional banks (Chase, BofA, Wells Fargo) would lag significantly.
