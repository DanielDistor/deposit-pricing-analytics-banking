## Key Observations

- The national average savings APY as of May 2026 is approximately 0.58–0.59%
- Top online banks are offering rates 7x the national average
- Rate compression has occurred: leading rates dropped from ~4.31% (Oct 2025) to ~4.00–4.03% (May 2026)
- The gap between online bank HYSAs and the national average remains enormous (~350 basis points)
- Traditional big banks like Chase (0.02%) and Citibank (0.03%) trail far behind (per Marcus comparison data)

## How to Choose a High-Yield Savings Account

1. Is the account FDIC insured?
2. Is the APY competitive?
3. How frequently is interest compounded?
4. What is the minimum balance or minimum opening deposit?
5. How is customer service and support availability?

## Ways to Use a High-Yield Savings Account

- Emergency fund building
- Saving for big purchases
- Long-term savings goals
- Safe place for retirement reserves
