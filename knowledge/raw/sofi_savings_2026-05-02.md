## About SoFi Bank

SoFi (Social Finance) started as a student loan refinancer and has grown into a comprehensive digital bank. SoFi Bank, N.A. is an FDIC-insured institution. The company went public in 2021 and offers banking, investing, loans, insurance, and credit score services. It operates without physical branches and passes savings to members through higher APYs.
