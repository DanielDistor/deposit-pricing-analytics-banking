## What Are CDs?

Certificates of deposit (CDs) are time-deposit accounts where:
1. You deposit a fixed amount for a fixed term (e.g., 6 months, 1 year, 5 years)
2. The bank pays a fixed interest rate for the duration
3. Early withdrawal typically incurs a penalty (varies widely by institution)
4. At maturity, you receive principal + interest

CDs are FDIC-insured up to $250,000 per depositor per bank. They are ideal for savers who want guaranteed returns and don't need immediate access to funds.
