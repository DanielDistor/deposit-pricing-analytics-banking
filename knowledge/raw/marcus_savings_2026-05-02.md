## About Marcus by Goldman Sachs

Marcus is the consumer banking division of Goldman Sachs, launched in 2016. It operates as Goldman Sachs Bank USA. Unlike Goldman Sachs's institutional business, Marcus specifically targets retail consumers with high-yield savings and personal loans. It has no physical branches and operates entirely online.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is a high-yield savings account?**
A savings account with a higher interest rate than traditional savings accounts. Interest is expressed as APY (annual percentage yield), accounting for compound interest.

**What are the best ways to use a high-yield savings account?**
- Emergency funds
- Saving for big purchases
- Long-term goals
- Retirement fund reserves
